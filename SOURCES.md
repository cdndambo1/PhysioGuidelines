# Clinical source register – MVP 1.0

Accessed / prepared: 16 November 2026. Always verify the live source before applying any recommendation.

## Neurology

- American Heart Association / American Stroke Association. **2026 Guideline for Adult Stroke Rehabilitation and Recovery**. APTA guideline resource record, dated 27 August 2026: https://www.apta.org/patient-care/evidence-based-practice-resources/cpgs/guidelines-for-adult-stroke-rehabilitation-and-recovery
- American Heart Association professional guidelines hub: https://professional.heart.org/en/guidelines-and-statements

## Musculoskeletal

- George SZ, Fritz JM, Silfies SP, et al. **Interventions for the Management of Acute and Chronic Low Back Pain: Revision 2021**. J Orthop Sports Phys Ther. 2021;51(11):CPG1–CPG60. DOI: https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2021.0304
