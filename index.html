EXPANDING THE LIBRARY

What the clinical library will cover

Planned condition coverage across the lifespan and care pathway. Only topics with a published summary are searchable as clinical guidelines.

Neurology and neurorehabilitation

Stroke; traumatic and acquired brain injury; spinal cord injury; multiple sclerosis; Parkinson’s disease; peripheral neuropathy; intellectual and developmental disabilities; cerebral palsy; spasticity and mobility.

Musculoskeletal and orthopaedics

Low back and neck pain; radiculopathy; osteoarthritis; tendinopathy; shoulder, hip, knee and ankle disorders; fractures; joint replacement; ligament and tendon surgery; persistent pain.

Paediatrics and development

Cerebral palsy; developmental delay; Down syndrome; spina bifida; developmental coordination disorder; torticollis; paediatric respiratory and neuromuscular conditions; transition to adult services.

Cardiorespiratory and critical care

COPD; asthma; bronchiectasis; pneumonia; cystic fibrosis; cardiac rehabilitation; heart failure; post-cardiac surgery; airway clearance; ICU-acquired weakness; early mobilisation and post-critical illness recovery.

Burns and wound-related rehabilitation

Acute burn rehabilitation; positioning and contracture prevention; scar and oedema management; splinting principles; mobility, function and return to participation.

Women’s and pelvic health

Stress, urge and mixed urinary incontinence; pelvic organ prolapse; pregnancy-related pelvic girdle pain; antenatal and postnatal rehabilitation; diastasis recti; pelvic pain and post-gynaecological surgery rehabilitation.

Older people, falls and frailty

Falls risk assessment; recurrent falls; frailty; deconditioning; osteoporosis; balance and gait impairment; sarcopenia; mobility after hospital admission; exercise for healthy ageing.

Prehabilitation and hospital transitions

Pre-operative assessment and exercise; peri-operative mobilisation; discharge planning; early supported discharge; post-hospital rehabilitation; long-term condition self-management and community reablement.

Oncology rehabilitation

Cancer-related fatigue; exercise during and after treatment; deconditioning; lymphoedema-related function; bone metastases precautions; rehabilitation after cancer surgery.

Amputee rehabilitation

Pre-operative and pre-prosthetic care; residual-limb management; contracture prevention; prosthetic gait; balance; falls; phantom limb pain considerations; community participation.

Vestibular rehabilitation

Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo; unilateral and bilateral vestibular hypofunction; persistent dizziness; balance impairment and vestibular migraine-related rehabilitation considerations.

Cross-cutting clinical practice

Outcome measurement; exercise prescription; pain science; disability and inclusion; intellectual disability; communication and supported decision-making; safeguarding; shared decision-making; health promotion and prevention.

This is a planned content map, not a statement that every condition already has a complete clinical guideline. Topic-specific summaries will be added after source verification and clinical review.

ABOUT THIS RESOURCE

Use evidence with clinical judgement.

PhysioGuidelines is an educational clinical reference prototype. It is not a medical device, diagnostic tool, patient record system or substitute for professional training and local clinical governance.

Summaries are intentionally concise and link to original sources. Always check the source guideline for full context, contraindications, population limits, evidence grading and updates before applying recommendations.

Irish context: The Irish Society of Chartered Physiotherapists (ISCP) is the professional body; CORU is the statutory regulator; the HSE is Ireland’s public health service. Their roles are distinct. This independent resource does not represent or imply endorsement by these organisations.

Content development: Each condition entry is intended to include assessment, red flags and referral, appropriate outcome measures, interventions and dosage/progression principles, MDT considerations, discharge/follow-up, original references and source evidence grading. New material remains labelled planned or draft until reviewed.

Do not enter patient-identifiable or confidential information. Bookmarks are stored locally in this browser.

Content status: Eleven source-linked clinical summaries across Neurology, Musculoskeletal, Paediatrics, Orthopaedics, Burns and Cardiorespiratory. New entries are educational summaries and have not yet undergone independent clinical peer review.
